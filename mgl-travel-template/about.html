About us

Eighteen years, one way of working

We started with a single walking route in the Alentejo and never changed the method: go first, then sell it.

See the trips

How a Wanderlane trip gets made

A guide proposes a route. They walk or drive it in the season we intend to sell, sleeping in the beds and eating in the places our travellers will. If it doesn't hold up, it doesn't launch.

What survives that gets costed whole — permits, transfers, guiding, most meals — so the price on the page is close to the price of the trip.

  • 2008 One route, six travellers, a borrowed van.
  • 2013 First guides on staff rather than contract.
  • 2019 Carbon accounting published per departure.
  • 2026 34 countries, still twelve seats.
