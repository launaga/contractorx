Wanderlane Travel — Changelog
=============================
Format: Keep a Changelog. Versioning: SemVer.


v1.0.0 — 2026-08-29
-------------------
Initial release.

Added
  - Six pages: index, about, services, service-details, portfolio, contact.
  - SCSS source split into _variables, _components and _responsive,
    compiling to assets/css/style.css and assets/css/responsive.css.
  - assets/js/main.js: sticky header, mobile nav, scroll reveal with GSAP
    fallback, animated counters, tour filtering, itinerary accordion and
    client-side form validation.
  - Vendored Bootstrap 5.3.3 and GSAP 3.12.5 (with ScrollTrigger).
  - documentation/index.html covering structure, build, tokens and JS hooks.
  - Placeholder media system: labelled gradients until real photography lands.

Notes
  - Forms are front-end only; wire initForms() to your own endpoint.
  - All demo copy, prices and departures are fictional.
