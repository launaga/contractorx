Portugal · 7 days

Azores Ridge Walk

Crater lakes, black-sand coves and a hot-spring dinner on the last night.

Request a seat All tours

The route

São Miguel end to end on foot, with three transfer days by van. Roughly 12–18 km a day on well-marked trail, one long day over the ridge, and afternoons free more often than not.

Arrival, kit check and dinner together. Nothing strenuous.

Twin crater lakes, 14 km on the rim, swim at the bottom if the wind allows.

Black sand, lava arches and the best bakery on the island.

The long one: 18 km and 900 m of ascent. Bags go ahead by van.

Steam vents, a lunch cooked in the ground, and a free afternoon.

Laurel forest, waterfall swim, hot-spring dinner to close it out.

Transfers timed to afternoon flights.
