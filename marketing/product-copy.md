**Why this kit exists**

A construction client's website has one job: survive a procurement review. That
review is somebody scrolling for proof — is the certification current, do they
own the plant, what is their safety record, are they already busy. Generic
themes answer none of it. You end up building those sections from scratch, on
every contractor project, for free.

This kit builds them once, properly:

- **Certifications wall** — ISO, national safety scheme, registration grade, and
  association memberships in a monochrome grid with validity dates. Reads at a
  glance.
- **Equipment fleet** — cards with unit counts, capacity, model, and year.
  Owned plant is the fastest credibility signal in construction.
- **Safety record** — LTIR and TRIR figures, incident-free-days counter, the
  safety programme, and room to be honest about the year something happened.
- **Ongoing projects** — inline SVG map plus progress bars. Signals live
  capacity without a map API key or a cookie banner.
- **Client logo strip** — named clients with their sector underneath.
- **Tender desk** — a separate submission flow with its own form, upload field,
  and a stated turnaround. Not a rebadged contact form.
- **Awards & press** — earned media stacked so it reads as a track record.

**The design**

Natural black and white. No colour accents anywhere — emphasis comes from
weight, scale, ruled space, and light-on-dark reversal used exactly three times
per page. Editorial serif for display, humanist sans for copy, mono for data
and labels. Blueprint dimension lines and tick corners as dividers. It looks
like a firm that builds things, not like a SaaS landing page with a hard hat
photo.

Dark mode inverts the whole palette and is included.

**FAQ**

*Can I use it for a client project?* Yes — one licence, one end product,
commercial use included. Charge whatever you like for the build.

*Do I need to know SCSS?* No. Text and image edits happen in `dist/` with no
tooling. SCSS is there when you want to rebrand.

*Is GSAP required?* No. It drives the hero reveal and the counters on the home
page only. Delete one script tag and both fall back gracefully.

*Can I resell it?* No. One end product per licence, and the kit itself can't be
redistributed. See LICENSE.

**Full description**

ContractorX solves a specific problem: generic contractor themes ship a hero, a
services grid, and a gallery, and stop there. A construction buyer needs proof —
current certifications, owned equipment, a safety record, and evidence of live
capacity. Those sections are in this template by default and built as real
components, not reskinned cards.

**Pages (7 + docs)**
1. Home — hero, stats band with counters, services, featured projects,
   certifications, clients, process, testimonial, CTA
2. About — story, milestones, team, values, safety record, certifications,
   awards & press
3. Services — six categorised services, scope comparison table, equipment
   fleet, process, FAQ, tender CTA
4. Service Detail — reusable template: hero, fact rail, scope list, capability
   spec table, assigned plant, programme, related projects, testimonial
5. Projects — featured strip, sector/year filters, portfolio grid, ongoing
   projects map, client strip
6. Project Detail — case study with fact rail, brief, scope, timeline, gallery,
   12-row as-built spec table, testimonial
7. Contact — validated form, six office locations, 24-hour emergency line,
   tender desk, location map
8. Documentation — twelve topics, included as a page

**Features**
- Bootstrap 5.3 grid and utilities with a custom SCSS layer
- 27 SCSS partials, one per section, compiling to a single stylesheet
- Seven design tokens control the entire palette; three control typography
- Dark mode via `prefers-color-scheme` plus a manual toggle
- Vanilla JS only — no jQuery. ~9 kB, or ~80 kB with optional GSAP
- Portfolio filtering by sector and year, no library
- Client-side form validation with focus management and disabled submit state
- Inline SVG maps — no third-party map service, no API key
- Three SVG sprites inlined at build time, so the template runs over `file://`
- SEO: Open Graph, Twitter cards, JSON-LD `GeneralContractor`, sitemap, robots
- Accessibility: skip link, landmarks, visible focus, ARIA, alt text,
  `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Responsive and tested at 320, 375, 414, 768, 1024, and 1440 px

**Requirements**
Any text editor for content edits. Node 18+ and one command if you want to
recompile the SCSS. No server-side language, no database, no CMS.

**What's included**
`dist/` (deployable site) · `src/` (HTML sources, partials, SCSS, JS, images,
icons) · documentation page · README with twelve edit guides · LICENSE ·
