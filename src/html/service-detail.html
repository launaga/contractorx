Scope What we deliver

  1. Site survey & setting outTotal-station control network, benchmark verification, and as-dug survey issued weekly.
  2. PilingBored piles to 1,200 mm and driven spun piles. PDA and CSL testing to SNI 8460:2017 on 2% of piles minimum.
  3. SubstructurePile caps, tie beams, raft foundations, and basement slabs with waterstop detailing.
  4. Retaining systemsSheet pile, secant pile, and soldier pile with ground anchors. Instrumented with inclinometers on deep excavations.
  5. Dewatering & drainageDeep-well and wellpoint dewatering, permanent stormwater, oil interceptors, and retention ponds.
  6. Hardstand & pavementRigid concrete pavement to 300 mm, heavy-duty yards, kerbs, and line marking.
