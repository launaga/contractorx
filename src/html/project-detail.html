01 · Brief

What the client needed.

Meranti Group had outgrown a leased facility in Bekasi and needed to move 340 staff and 26,000 pallet positions into a building they owned — without a gap in distribution. The site was a former paddy field with soft clay to 9 m, and a live warehouse operated on the adjoining plot.

The brief set three conditions: a fixed lump-sum price at tender, 12 m clear internal height for double-deep racking, and no interruption to the neighbouring operation. A fourth arrived at week 14 — a 6,000 m² mezzanine — absorbed without moving the hand-over date.

02 · Scope delivered
  • Vibro-replacement soil improvement over 4.2 ha
  • 1,180 driven spun piles, PDA tested on 2.4%
  • 250 mm laser-screeded ground slab, FM2, 8 t/m²
  • 4,100 t structural steel frame, 12 m clear height
  • Insulated envelope, 34 dock levellers with shelters
  • Full MEP — LV, LED high-bay, ESFR sprinklers
  • 1,900 m² office fit-out, joinery from Cikupa
  • 3.1 ha hardstand, gatehouse, weighbridge, retention
