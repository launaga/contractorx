04 · Before you tender

The six questions
estimators ask first.

01

What project value do you take on?

LPJK Grade 7 carries no upper limit. In practice our range is IDR 40 – 600 billion. Below IDR 15 billion we usually recommend a specialist subcontractor instead of bidding.

+
02

How fast can you mobilise?

Fourteen days from letter of intent inside Greater Jakarta, 21 days elsewhere in Java, 30 days for Sumatra, Batam, and Johor. Plant availability is confirmed in writing before we sign.

+
03

Do you self-perform or subcontract?

Structure, civil works, and formwork are self-performed. MEP, façade, and lift installation run through a fixed panel used since 2016 — no open tendering mid-project.

+
04

What does your reporting look like?

Weekly: S-curve against baseline, three-week look-ahead, photographic progress log, RFI register, open change orders. Monthly: a valuation certified by the QS and a rolling risk register.

+
05

How are variations priced?

Against the schedule of rates in the contract. Anything outside it is measured quantity plus an agreed percentage — set at tender stage, never negotiated mid-build.

+
06

What happens after hand-over?

Twenty-four months of defect liability with a two-working-day response for anything affecting operations, plus O&M training for your facilities team in the final month on site.
