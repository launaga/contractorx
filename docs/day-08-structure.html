Photography treatment — duotone black-on-warm-white

Plates above are CSS stand-ins for the tonal range and framing — final assets are real site photography, duotoned #0A0A0A → #FAFAF8, exported AVIF + WebP.

Rules of the direction
Do
  • Emphasis by weight, scale, ruled space, reversal — nothing else.
  • Blueprint dimension lines and tick corners as dividers.
  • Dense grids — trust signals stack, hairlines separate.
  • Wide, uncropped, industrial framing. Scale is the story.
  • Motion: hero reveal + one counter run-up. prefers-reduced-motion honoured.
Don't
  • Any colour accent, including hover states and form focus rings.
  • More than three reversal blocks on a page.
  • Parallax, bounce easing, scroll-jacking, marquees.
  • Stock handshakes, hi-vis portraits, aerial city shots.
  • All-caps body copy or colour-highlighted words.
