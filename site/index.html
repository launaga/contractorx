§01 · Problem

Small contractors run million-rupiah projects on WhatsApp, Excel, and a folded BOQ in the glovebox.

The bigger firms have Procore. Homeowners doing a single renovation have nothing but their contractor's word. In between — the 300,000+ small contractors that actually build South-East Asia — is a tooling desert. Every problem below is a real one, sourced from 34 discovery interviews.

73% of quotes lose money

Quotes are built from memory or a two-year-old Excel; margins evaporate before the first pour of concrete.

21d avg. time-to-first-invoice

Progress claims sit in a notebook until the weekend. Cashflow stalls; workers are paid from personal savings.

4.2 tools per project

WhatsApp, Excel, Google Photos, printed BOQ, occasional Trello. No source of truth; disputes are word-vs-word.

0 clients happy with visibility

Owners get a weekly voice note. They pay tens of millions of rupiah on faith and screenshots.

Insight · The pain is coordination, not craftsmanship.
§02 · Product

One thread from lead to last-payment — every project, every crew, every photo.

ContractorX collapses seven tools into one workspace tuned to how a small contractor actually operates: mobile-first for the field, desktop-clean for the office, and a read-only mirror for the client so they stop asking "sudah sampai mana, Pak?"

F-01

Quote & BOQ Builder

Core · v1.0 Template Export
Drag rooms and scopes onto a canvas; ContractorX pulls unit rates from your own library or the seeded SNI reference. Every line has a live margin badge. Export a branded PDF the client will actually sign.
  • Row-level margin, project-level margin, running total
  • Reusable line-item library per user
  • Version history — every re-negotiation is a diff, not a new file
F-02

Project Timeline & Gates

Core · v1.0 Gantt-lite
A stripped-down Gantt with named milestone gates. Gates are what unlock progress-based invoicing — a gate is only "done" when the client taps approve in their portal (or 72h auto-approve, contractor's choice).
  • Weather-day buffer built into every timeline
  • Critical-path highlighted, delay ripples visualised
  • Gate approvals trigger F-04 invoice draft automatically
F-03

Daily Site Log

Core · v1.0 Mobile Offline
The mandor opens the app, snaps 6 photos, ticks the crew count, notes weather, submits. Log is timestamped, geo-stamped, and the client sees a curated feed inside their portal. Works offline; syncs when signal returns.
  • Photo compression on-device to survive 3G upload
  • Voice-to-text notes (Bahasa Indonesia + regional)
  • Auto-generated end-of-week summary PDF
F-04

Progress-based Invoicing

Core · v1.0 Payments
When a gate is approved, ContractorX drafts the invoice at the agreed percentage of contract value. One tap sends it via WhatsApp with a Xendit /Midtrans payment link. Money lands, ledger updates, payroll unlocks.
  • Retention (retensi) auto-calculated per contract
  • PPN 11% toggle per line, per project
  • Overdue nudges — three touches, then escalation to owner
F-05

Crew & Sub-contractor

v1.0 Payroll
One roster across projects. Assign day-rates or scope-rates, auto-calculate weekly payroll from the site log's attendance ticks, settle via bank transfer batch. Sub-contractors get read-only access to just their scope.
F-06

Client Portal

Core · v1.0 Read-only
A magic-link web view for the homeowner: timeline, gate approvals, photo feed, invoices. No login friction. Every open logged — you know when they've seen it.
F-07

Playbook Templates

v1.1
Ship with 12 curated templates — bedah rumah 60m², interior apartemen, renovasi kamar mandi, dst. Each is a full BOQ + timeline + gate structure. New user reaches "first sent quote" in under 15 minutes.

§06 · Architecture

Boring stack. Interesting product.

The novel work is at the seam between BOQ, gate, and invoice — not in the infrastructure. Every choice below is optimised for a two-engineer team shipping in 22 weeks.

Data model — first cut
orgs · users · memberships · projects · scopes · line_items · quotes · quote_versions · gates · logs · log_photos · invoices · payments · payouts · crew_members · attendance
Auth model
phone-OTP for contractor + mandor · magic-link (email/WA) for client · RLS scoped by org_id and role · client sees only their project rows
Offline strategy
daily-log form is a client-side draft in IndexedDB; photos queued with resumable uploads; sync on connectivity + on app foreground
Payment split
Xendit primary (VA + e-wallet + QRIS); Midtrans switch if VA success-rate under 92% for 24h; webhook double-write to ledger
Cost envelope · steady state
≈ Rp 3.8M / mo at 500 active orgs · Supabase Pro + Vercel Pro + Trigger + WA + payments processor fees
Compliance
UU PDP alignment · encryption at rest · signed audit log for gate approvals and payments
