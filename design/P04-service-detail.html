01 · Scope

What we deliver.

  • 01
    Site survey & setting out

    Total-station control network, benchmark verification, as-dug survey issued weekly.

  • 02
    Piling

    Bored piles to 1,200 mm and driven spun piles. PDA and CSL testing to SNI 8460:2017 on 2% minimum.

  • 03
    Substructure

    Pile caps, tie beams, raft foundations, and basement slabs with waterstop detailing.

  • 04
    Retaining systems

    Sheet pile, secant pile, soldier pile with ground anchors. Inclinometers on deep excavations.

  • 05
    Dewatering & drainage

    Deep-well and wellpoint dewatering, permanent stormwater, interceptors, retention ponds.

  • 06
    Hardstand & pavement

    Rigid concrete pavement to 300 mm, heavy-duty yards, kerbs, and line marking.
