Scope of work

A typical Ridgeline kitchen runs eight weeks from demo to final inspection. Layout changes that move plumbing or a load-bearing wall add two to three weeks and an engineering review. Selections are locked before demo — that single rule is why our schedules hold.

Measured drawings, cabinet layout, and every finish chosen and ordered. Nothing is demolished until long-lead items have a confirmed delivery date.

Plan review submitted and tracked by us. Structural changes go to an engineer first; you get the stamped set for your records.

Containment walls, floor protection and lead testing on pre-1978 homes. Two to three days, dumpster included.

Framing, electrical, plumbing and HVAC. Photographed before insulation so you know what is inside your walls.

Level 4 finish as standard, level 5 on request. Primed and painted before cabinets land.

Cabinets set and scribed, counters templated on site, backsplash and flooring by our own tile crew.

Final inspection, walkthrough with your superintendent, warranty documents and appliance manuals handed over.
