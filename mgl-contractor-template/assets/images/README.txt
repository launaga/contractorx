Drop project photography here.

Naming used by the demo markup:
  hero-<slug>.jpg      1920x1080, hero backgrounds
  service-<slug>.jpg   1200x800, service cards (3:2)
  team-<slug>.jpg      600x600, team portraits (1:1)
  project-<n>.jpg      1200x800, projects grid

Until real images are added, .card-media renders a flat panel with the
data-img label so layouts stay measurable. Replace it with a
background-image (or swap the div for an <img>) once assets land.

Jobsite photography tip: shoot the rough-in stage too. Framing, flashing
and waterproofing photos convert better than finished-kitchen shots
because nobody else publishes them.
