About us

Twenty-two years, same crew philosophy

We started as a two-man framing outfit and never outgrew the habit of doing the structural work ourselves.

See our services

How a Ridgeline job runs

One superintendent owns your project from permit to punch list. They are on site daily, they hold the schedule, and they are the only number you need.

Framing, finish carpentry and tile are self-performed — the trades where shortcuts hide. Electrical, plumbing and HVAC go to the same three subcontractors we have used for over a decade, on our sequence, not theirs.

Every job gets a written schedule at contract signing and weekly progress photos. When something changes, you hear it from us before you see it on an invoice.

  • 2004 Founded as a two-man framing crew.
  • 2009 General contractor license; first whole-home renovation.
  • 2016 In-house tile and finish carpentry shop opens.
  • 2021 Ten-year written workmanship warranty introduced.
  • 2026 480 projects; 31 people on staff.
